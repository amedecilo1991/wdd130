Whitewater Rapids Co.

Our mission is to deliver safe, unforgettable, and thrilling river adventures that connect people with nature and build lifelong memories. Whether you're a beginner or a veteran paddler, our expert guides ensure an amazing experience on the water.

History

Founded over two decades ago by a small team of passionate river guides, Whitewater Rapids Co. started with just two rafts and a shared dream of sharing wild rivers safely with adventurers of all skill levels.

Over the years, we have grown from a humble local outfitter into a premier destination for river exploration, upholding top-tier safety standards, environmental stewardship, and unforgettable outdoor memories.
