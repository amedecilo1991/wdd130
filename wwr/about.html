Whitewater Rapids Co.

Our mission is to deliver safe, unforgettable, and thrilling river adventures that connect people with nature and build lifelong memories. Whether you're a beginner or a veteran paddler, our expert guides ensure an amazing experience on the water.

History

Founded over two decades ago by a small team of passionate river guides, Whitewater Rapids Co. has grown from a humble local outfitter into a premier destination for river exploration. We pride ourselves on top-tier safety standards, environmental stewardship, and sharing our love for the great outdoors with every guest who steps into a raft.
